Rubiks' cubes

How to solve a 3x3 cube?

the simplest way to solve a 3x3 is useing the begginer method.

  1. Create the white cross
  2. Insert the white corners (this should complete the first/white layer)
  3. Put the side edges
  4. Make the yellow edges point up
  5. Arrange the corners
  6. make the corners point up

Ways to solve a 3x3 cube

the begginer method is the simplest one, but the are more faster methods.

  • Begginer Method
  • CFOP
  • Roux
  • ZZ(most uncommon)
